# Code of Conduct

OpenFriend contributors are expected to make the project welcoming, thoughtful,
and safe.

## Our standard

Be respectful. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people. Welcome
questions, different experience levels, and disagreement offered in good faith.
Protect private information. Do not use sexualized language, harassment,
threats, discrimination, doxxing, or sustained disruption.

Because OpenFriend concerns intimate conversation and emotional interaction,
take reports about privacy, manipulation, dependency, consent, and safety
seriously.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at
`theandrewsiah+openfriend@gmail.com`. The maintainer may remove content,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation according
to severity and pattern. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

This compact policy is adapted from the intent of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
